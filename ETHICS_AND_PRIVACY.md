# JDCL Ethical, Privacy, and Governance Safeguards

1. **Prohibition on Judge Ranking & Outcome Prediction:** JDCL strictly prohibits building or submitting models that rank individual judges, profile judicial panels, or predict individual judicial decisions.
2. **Zero Personal Identifying Information (PII):** All challenge datasets and notebooks must contain aggregated, anonymized, or synthetic records only.
3. **No Restricted or Unlicensed Case Data:** Publicly accessible decisions may carry redistribution restrictions; never redistribute raw scraped judgments without verified provenance and licensing clearance.
4. **Human Approval Gate:** No challenge, dataset, or competition package may be switched from Private to Public on Kaggle without explicit human sign-off.
5. **Non-Monetary Recognition:** All JDCL v1.0-ZC challenges award non-monetary Kaggle Kudos and contributor attribution only (Rp 0 cash prizes).
