# JFLM 2027 Competition FAQ

**Q: Is this real court data?**  
No. All 10,000 rows in `jflm_2027.csv` are 100% synthetic data generated deterministically (`seed=20270101`).

**Q: Are there cash prizes?**  
No. Under the JDCL Zero-Cost Community Mandate (`v1.0-ZC`), recognition is provided via public leaderboards, open-source participation certificates, and collaborative research publication opportunities.

**Q: Do I need a GPU?**  
No. All baselines and validation pipelines run on standard CPU hardware or free Kaggle Notebooks within quota.
