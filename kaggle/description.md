# Justice for the Last Mile Challenge 2027 (JFLM 2027)

> **SYNTHETIC DATA NOTICE:** This Kaggle Community Competition uses **100% synthetic data** (`SYNTHETIC_JFLM_2027_V1`). It contains no real citizen identities, no real litigant records, and no real judicial cases.

## Problem Statement

Across archipelagic and rural regions, last-mile access to justice faces logistical barriers including distance to courts, travel cost, digital connectivity gaps, and legal-aid capacity constraints. JFLM 2027 invites participants to model **service delivery duration (`resolution_days`)** and **fairness across geographic and vulnerability subgroups** to surface testable hypotheses for improving legal-aid and mobile-court allocation.

## Non-Automation Principle

This challenge strictly models **service delivery logistics**. It does **not** predict verdicts, sentencing, or automate judicial decision-making.
