# Competition Rules — JFLM 2027

1. **Community Competition Only:** Zero-cost Kaggle Community Competition; no cash prizes or paid promotion.
2. **Open-Source Reproducibility:** Top-ranked solutions must include reproducible CPU-executable notebooks and fixed random seeds.
3. **No External Real Judicial Data:** Participants must not attempt to link synthetic records to real individuals or courts.
4. **No Judicial Decision Automation:** Models must strictly address service delivery logistics.
5. **Responsible AI:** Solutions must report subgroup fairness diagnostics alongside overall MAE.
